💡 Remember before you play

  • Vowels: a, e, i, o, u. The other 21 letters are consonants.
  • Blend the sounds: c + at → cat, h + en → hen, p + ig → pig.
  • Word families rhyme: cat, hat, mat (-at) · pin, tin, bin (-in) · bug, mug, rug (-ug).
  • Short a cat · short e pet · short i sit · short o hot · short u sun.
  • Say the sound, not the letter name!
